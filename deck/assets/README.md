# Deck assets

`aiori_logo.png` is the AIORI logo exactly as the organisers' template provides it. It was taken from page 1
of `Proposed-structure-hackathon.pdf` at the repository root. The RGB image (120 × 119 px) and its soft
mask were joined into one RGBA PNG. The pixels were not rescaled, recoloured or redrawn. It is not our
artwork. It appears on the title and closing slides, placed as on the proposal's title page.
